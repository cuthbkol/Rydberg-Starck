A community-first approach

Learning that starts with people

Working with district councils, Rydberg Starck delivered practical training designed for community needs. Participants built digital confidence, explored ways to strengthen their businesses and learned skills they could put to use in everyday life.

The project brought digital learning closer to communities across Southern Malawi, with a focus on access, inclusion and learning by doing.
