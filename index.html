WHO WE ARE

Bridging the
Digital Divide

Rydberg Starck Limited is a dynamic innovation-driven tech company established in May 2022, with operations beginning in January 2023. We tackle the societal challenge of the digital divide in Malawi by providing comprehensive digital solutions.

Our ecosystem combines cutting-edge digital technology, engineering prowess, and modern artistic expression to create transformative solutions. We believe in the power of inclusive digital platforms that empower youth, communities, and institutions.
