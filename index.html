Who we are

Bridging the digital divide. Building what comes next.

Rydberg Starck is a Malawi-based, innovation-driven technology company. Since beginning operations in 2023, we have combined digital solutions, engineering, and creative thinking to help people and institutions move forward.

We believe useful technology should make opportunity more accessible—and should be shaped around the people who use it.
